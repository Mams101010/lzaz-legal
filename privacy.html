Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Lzaz Technology Company ("Lzaz", "we", "us", or "our") operates the Lzaz Sports mobile application. This page explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account data: name, email address, phone number, profile picture, and username when you register.
  • Location data: approximate or precise location when you create or search for matches nearby. Location is only accessed with your permission.
  • Usage data: match history, team activity, connections, and in-app interactions.
  • Device data: device type, OS version, push notification tokens for delivering alerts, and crash reports that help us fix problems.
  • Apple Health data (optional, stays on your phone): if you allow it, the app reads workouts your Apple Watch recorded, to show you your own match stats. This data never leaves your phone. See section 5.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate the app and provide core features (match creation, player connections, team management).
  • To send push notifications about matches, invitations, and updates you have opted into.
  • To show you your own stats for matches you played, from Apple Health, if you allow it. This happens only on your phone.
  • To improve the app through aggregated, anonymised analytics.
  • To enforce our community guidelines and prevent abuse.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Other users as part of normal app functionality (e.g. your profile is visible to players you connect with).
  • Cloud service providers (AWS, MongoDB Atlas) that host our infrastructure under strict data-processing agreements.
  • Firebase (Google) for push notification delivery and anonymised app analytics.
  • Sentry for crash reports that help us fix bugs.

None of these, and no one else, ever receives your Apple Health data.

4. Location Permissions

Location access is used to show nearby matches and venues. We do not share your precise location with other users. If you allow Apple Health access, the route your Apple Watch recorded during a match is used on your phone to draw your heat map, and is never sent to us (see section 5). You may revoke location permission at any time in your device settings.

5. Apple Health and Apple Watch

Lzaz Sports can show you your own stats for a match you played in: distance, time played, calories, average speed, average heart rate, and a heat map of where you played. It uses the workouts your Apple Watch recorded. This is optional and stays off until you allow it.

  • What the app reads: workouts, workout routes, heart rate, and active energy (calories) from Apple Health, only for the hours around a match you played in. The app never writes anything to Apple Health.
  • It stays on your phone: your stats are worked out on your phone each time you open them. Lzaz never sends Apple Health data to its servers, never stores it, and never puts it in iCloud.
  • Only you see it: it is never shown to other players, never added to your profile, and never shared with anyone.
  • No advertising or marketing: Lzaz never uses Apple Health data for advertising, marketing, or data mining, never sells it, and never gives it to third parties, including analytics and crash-reporting providers.
  • Turning it off: you can stop access at any time in the Health app (tap your picture, then Apps, then Lzaz) or in Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › Lzaz.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account data while your account is active. You may delete your account at any time from within the app, which permanently removes your personal data within 30 days. Lzaz does not keep Apple Health data at all (see section 5).

7. Children's Privacy

Lzaz Sports is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures including encrypted connections (TLS), hashed passwords, and access controls to protect your data.

9. Your Rights

You may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
